Fasting Activity Tracker Support

Help with fasting, activity, mileage, weight, water, reminders, and Apple Health.

Contact

Email hershapplications@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email private health information.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. Open Profile in the app and choose Connect Apple Health. You can change access later in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Reminders

Enable reminders in Profile. If they do not appear, open iOS Settings → Notifications → Fasting Activity Tracker and confirm notifications are allowed.

Delete app data

Open Profile and choose Delete All App Data. Entries previously written to Apple Health remain under your control in the Health app.
